AeroGen

AeroGen is my dissertation research project building an autonomous aerodynamic design pipeline for Formula 1 car geometries under the FIA 2026 regulations. The first stage, now completed, is a procedural reconstruction of the FIA 2026 reference box car: a full regulation-compliant geometry encoding more than 35 reference volumes from the technical regulations using CSG boolean operations and plane trims. This envelope defines the legal boundary that every generated design must satisfy.

The next stage constructs a parametric F1 car inside this envelope, controlled by a design parameter dictionary. Sampling the parameter space programmatically will generate a large dataset of regulation-compliant geometry variations, labeled with aerodynamic performance data from CFD simulation. This dataset will then be used to train surrogate models that can rapidly predict aerodynamic performance from geometry, reducing the need to run computationally expensive CFD simulations at every design point and enabling faster exploration of the aerodynamic design space.

Beyond Idealized Voids: Linking Pore Morphology to Hotspot Sensitivity

This project investigates how realistic pore morphology influences shock-induced hotspot formation in reactive materials. We construct a Statistical Shape Model from 134 pore geometries, representing shape variation through 20 interpretable principal components that capture 97.5% of the morphological variance. The resulting latent space connects directly to geometric features including elongation, curvature, asymmetry, and orientation.

Novel pore geometries sampled from this shape space are evaluated using LatentPARC, a physics-aware neural surrogate that predicts the evolving temperature field during pore collapse. By linking interpretable morphology parameters to hotspot quantities such as hotspot area, temperature, and their rates of change, the project aims to identify which specific geometric features most strongly control material sensitivity and provide a more systematic alternative to idealized pore shapes or uninterpretable generative models.

rcsb-api

A Python package for programmatic access to the RCSB Protein Data Bank — home to over 230,000 3D structures of proteins, nucleic acids, and macromolecular machines. The package wraps the RCSB Search and Data APIs in a Pythonic interface, allowing researchers to query, retrieve, and integrate structural biology data without needing to understand the underlying GraphQL schema. Published in Journal of Molecular Biology (2025) .

HPC-ED

HPC-ED is a federated catalog that aggregates training materials — tutorials, workshops, and course modules — from partner institutions across the HPC and cyberinfrastructure community. As part of the project, I built a proof-of-concept automated quality assurance system that uses a two-stage LLM pipeline to crawl catalog entries, including YouTube transcript extraction, summarize content, and score metadata accuracy against the actual resource.

OmniPARC

OmniPARC is a physics-aware neural surrogate model for shock-compressed solid reactive materials. The project works toward foundation models for energetic materials by learning shared dynamics across different chemical species and microstructural geometries, enabling prediction and analysis of chemistry–structure–performance relationships.
